A little about me

I started with both hardware and software.

I've always been the person who wants to know what's happening underneath the surface.

Working with technology has given me the chance to approach problems from both sides. I enjoy understanding how a machine works, but I also want to understand the software running on it and what happens when the two don't behave the way they should.

At Apple, I troubleshoot hardware and software issues, reproduce problems, narrow down possible causes, test solutions, document what I find, and help customers and teammates work through technical problems.

My M.S. in Information Technology has helped me build on that experience through software development, operating systems, systems analysis, and other areas of information technology. I'm especially interested in QA and testing, hardware-software interaction, and building things myself so I can understand them from the ground up.
